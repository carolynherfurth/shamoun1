The Difference

I've been collecting
beauty my whole life.
Now I deliver it.

Most florists have a formula. Skyline Garden has a point of view. Every arrangement Shamoun creates carries the eye of someone who has moved between continents, absorbed color and form from cultures most people only visit, and spent twenty years looking out at one of the most dramatic skylines on earth.

The result is arrangements that feel unexpected — combinations that shouldn't work, and stop the room when they do. Not paint-by-numbers. Not safe. Something that makes you look twice and keeps you looking.

The Story

I live forty floors above 42nd Street. I look out at the Empire State Building every day.

And for years, I've filled my home with arrangements that match what I see out that window — unexpected, alive, a little bit stunning.

Friends would come over and ask: who does your flowers? I'd say: I do. And then I'd show up at their place and do theirs too — armfuls of something they hadn't seen before, arranged right there, for the room, for the light, for the person who lived there.

After thirty years in finance — and a lifetime moving between Jordan, Montreal, California, and New York — I realized I'd been collecting beauty everywhere I went. It was time to share it.

The Name

Skyline — the energy and ambition of the city. Garden — the beauty and stillness of nature. Both, inside your home.

The Eye

Born in Jordan. Schooled in Montreal and California. Twenty years in Manhattan. Every place left something — a color, a form, a way of seeing.

How It Works

Simple.
Unhurried.
Extraordinary.

This is a relationship, not a transaction. Shamoun takes on a small number of clients at a time — residential and corporate — to ensure every arrangement receives the attention it deserves.

1
We Talk

A brief conversation about your space, your eye, what moves you. Or you tell Shamoun nothing and trust him entirely. Both work beautifully.

2
He Creates

Each arrangement is considered, not fulfilled. Sourced for the season. Composed for your room. Never a standard order.

3
It Arrives

Delivered to your door — sometimes arranged on the spot. Always an entrance. Recurring weekly, biweekly, or monthly — or one-time with 2-day notice.

Tier 01
Deluxe

Elegant, high-quality arrangements. For residential and corporate clients who want consistent, considered beauty.

Tier 02
Premium Deluxe
