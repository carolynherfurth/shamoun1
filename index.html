The Story

The city and nature,
brought into equilibrium.

The Skyline represents the big city — its energy, its scale, its ambition.
The Garden represents nature — its beauty, its tranquility, its stillness.
Skyline Garden's arrangements bring both together, creating a sense of harmony in the spaces you return to.

Shamoun lives on the 44th floor above 42nd Street, looking out at the Empire State Building every day. For years, he has filled his home with arrangements that match what he sees out that window — unexpected, alive, a little bit stunning. In Manhattan, his living room is his garden. There is always an arrangement — often several — and everyone who walks in feels it.

The arrangements he makes for clients carry the same attention he has always given to the people closest to him — for the room, for the energy, for the person who lives there. Six continents. Thirty years of absorbing. Every place left something — a color, a form, a way of seeing. For those who want their home to reflect not just taste, but a point of view shaped by the world.

The Experience

Tailored.
Thoughtful.
Extraordinary.

Skyline Garden cultivates an elevated, lasting relationship with each client — residential and corporate — built on beauty, care, and an uncompromising standard for what a space can feel like.

Browse the collection and select the world that speaks to you — Peonies, Shades of Yellow, Tropical, and more.

2

Before dawn, Shamoun is at the New York flower markets — knowing which vendors to seek out, carefully choosing each stem. Each arrangement is then composed by hand, at home, for the person who will receive it.
